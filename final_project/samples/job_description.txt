AI and LLM Research Software Engineer Intern

We are seeking an undergraduate research software engineer intern to help build reliable evaluation systems for large language models and data-intensive AI applications. The intern will work with researchers and engineers to design reproducible experiments, process large datasets, analyze model behavior, and communicate findings through clear technical documentation.

What you will do
- Build Python data pipelines for large-scale data processing, web data collection, filtering, annotation, and quality auditing
- Design and implement evaluation frameworks for large language models, including repeated sampling, consistency analysis, confidence analysis, and failure-mode evaluation
- Develop reproducible experiments for AI systems operating under incomplete context, ambiguity, temporal uncertainty, and rule-based constraints
- Use Linux, Docker, and shared research computing environments to run parallel data-processing and model-evaluation workflows
- Analyze experimental results and identify cases where language models produce confident but incorrect or unsupported decisions
- Build internal research dashboards and data-inspection interfaces using JavaScript, TypeScript, React, or Next.js
- Collaborate with researchers and software engineers to translate research requirements into maintainable tools
- Prepare technical reports, experiment documentation, and research presentations

What we are looking for
- Currently pursuing a bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Data Science, or a related field
- Experience programming in Python and working with structured datasets
- Familiarity with large language models, AI evaluation, prompt design, annotation workflows, or model reliability research
- Experience with large-scale data processing, web crawling, rule-based filtering, or quality auditing
- Working knowledge of Linux, Git, Docker, and collaborative software development practices
- Ability to analyze experimental results and explain technical findings clearly
- Strong attention to correctness, reproducibility, privacy, and responsible AI practices

Preferred qualifications
- Experience with React, TypeScript, Next.js, SQL, or interactive data visualization
- Experience using shared or cloud research computing resources for parallel workloads
- Familiarity with confidence metrics, entropy, majority voting, consistency measurement, or error analysis
- Prior academic research, technical writing, or cross-functional product development experience
